Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Aerlis – Train your breath is a mobile application designed to help users improve breath control and performance.

Information We Collect

Aerlis does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The app uses microphone input only locally on your device to detect breath activity. No audio is recorded or transmitted.

Microphone Permission

The app requires microphone access to detect breath activity during training sessions. All processing happens locally.

Data Storage

Training data such as duration and attempts may be stored locally on your device. This data is never transmitted.

Third-Party Services

Aerlis does not use analytics, ads, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Aerlis does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
