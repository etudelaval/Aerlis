# Privacy Policy
# Last updated: 2026

Aerlis – Train your breath is a mobile application designed to help users improve breath control and performance through simple training sessions.

This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information We Collect

Aerlis does **not collect, store, or transmit any personal information**.

The app uses audio input from the device microphone **only locally on the user's device** to detect breath activity and measure duration during training sessions.

No audio recordings are stored or transmitted.

## Microphone Permission

The app requires access to the device microphone to detect breath activity during training.

All audio data is processed in real time and remains entirely on the device.

## Data Storage

Training session data such as:

- longest breath duration  
- number of attempts  
- total effective training time  

may be stored locally on the user's device to provide progress tracking.

This data is not transmitted to any external server.

## Third-Party Services

Aerlis does **not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services**.

## Children's Privacy

Aerlis is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page.
